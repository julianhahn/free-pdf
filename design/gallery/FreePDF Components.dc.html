FreePDF · iPhone, portrait · iOS 18 and up

Component specifications

Twenty components, each named as the code will name it, in every state, light and dark. Sizes are in points at the Large (default) Dynamic Type setting; nothing is given a fixed height, so larger settings grow the component rather than clipping it. Every touch target is at least 44×44 pt. Every label leaves room for German, which runs about 30% longer.

A scan is made of pages, a page starts as a photo, the finished result is the PDF. No control is icon-only. Nothing here implies a network.
