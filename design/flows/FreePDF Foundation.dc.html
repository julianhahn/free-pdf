The screens themselves are in the flow sheets. Nothing here changes a colour, a face, a size step, a spacing step, a radius or a shadow, except the one place the brief reopens: disabled.

F1

The disabled fix — a colour role, not an opacity

A flat 45% opacity drops a real instruction — “Photograph at least one page” — below comfortable reading contrast, and it fades the border and the shape along with the label. The proposal keeps the shape and the hairline at full strength, removes the accent, and prints the label in a defined disabled text colour.

--disabled-opacityretired — set to 1; opacity is no longer the mechanism

What still says “dead”: the accent is gone (a live primary is accent-stroked, a dead one is a grey hairline), the destructive double rule is gone with it, the switch track stays at divider-strong with the knob unmoved, and the slider thumb loses its accent fill. Shape and stroke carry the state; faintness no longer has to.

Spec

  • Add --disabled-text, --disabled-border, --disabled-surface to tokens/colors.css in both themes; add the viewfinder overrides under a .fp-on-dark ground class.
  • In every component, replace opacity: disabled ? var(--disabled-opacity) : 1 with color: var(--disabled-text), borderColor: var(--disabled-border), and — where a variant carries the accent (Button primary/ghost/destructive, Switch on, Slider thumb, Shutter ring) — drop the accent to --disabled-border too.   • --disabled-opacity stays defined at 1 so nothing breaks while clients migrate, then is deleted.

F2

New · PageStrip

Asked for in S15. 40 pages are not 40 swipes: the rail is how a page is reached, and the jump is how page 37 is reached without dragging. PageImage is the tile; nothing in the kit scrolls them or jumps to a number.

Rail, page 3 of 12 selected
Rail of 40, page 5 refused, jump open

PageStrip — spec

Built from: PageImage (40 px wide tiles, label = page number, selected, state, grey), Button secondary “Go to page” for the jump, Button secondary for “Go”. No new colour, no new size: the tile is the existing tile, the divider before the jump control is the existing hairline.

Props pages[{n,state,src}], selected, onSelect, total, grey, jump: "closed" | "open", jumpValue, onJumpChange, onJumpSubmit.

States selected tile · refused tile (inherited from PageImage) · grey on · jump closed · jump open · rail scrolled (selected tile is kept in view when the page changes from a swipe).

Order and spacing rail and jump control in one row, space-2 between tiles, space-2 padding and a 1 px divider left of the jump control; space-2 between the row and the open jump field.

VoiceOver rail: “Pages, 3 of 12 shown”; each tile: “Page 3, shown” / “Page 5, could not be scanned”; jump button: “Go to page. Go to a page number. Page 3 of 12 shown”; field: “Page number, 1 to 40”; Go: “Go to page 37”.

F3

New · ToolStrip

Asked for in S23. Six tools, one active, always visible. Tag is a state chip and cannot be selected; MenuList opens and closes.

Edges active Brightness active

ToolStrip — spec

Built from: Icon (17 px) plus the control type step. Active = accent glyph, accent label and the 2 px accent underline — 2 px is already the system’s functional weight (focus ring, shutter ring, viewfinder corners). Passive = text-muted, no rule.

Props items[{label,icon}], active, onSelect. States active · passive · pressed (press-neutral) · focused (2 px accent ring, 2 px outside) · scrolled (six tools do not fit at large text sizes, so the row scrolls horizontally and never wraps or shrinks).

Order and spacing one row, space-2 between tools, space-2 horizontal padding inside each tool, min height 44 pt, a hairline above and below the strip.

VoiceOver strip: “Adjustment tools”; each: “Edges” / “Edges, shown”. Never “tab”, never “selected chip”.

F4

New · PageHandles

Asked for in S24. Edges and Crop both drag corners over a page, and Crop refuses a box that does not fit. Drawn once, with the refusal as a state.

Four handles — Edges
Eight handles, refused — Crop

PageHandles — spec

Built from: PageImage underneath (unchanged), plus an overlay of 1 px accent edge and 11 px drawn handles in 44 pt targets, each seated on a 2 px --paper ring so a handle stays visible on a dark page. Refused: the edge and handles go to --destructive and the edge gains the inset ring — the same double rule that makes a destructive button a different shape — with the sentence under the picture. Never colour alone.

Props src, grey, count: 4 | 8, inset, refused, refusedText. States resting · handle held (handle grows to 13 px, no colour change) · refused.

VoiceOver each handle: “Top left corner of the sheet. Swipe up or down to move it.” Refused: the sentence is announced as an alert once, not per drag.

Open question is the refusal sentence above the engine’s, or does the engine own it? If the engine has a string for it, send it and this one goes.

F5

New · Sheet

Asked for in S36. ConfirmDialog is the only raised surface and it is a dialog: title, body, two actions. The reader needs a raised surface that is a close control and content, nothing else.

Sheet — spec

Surface, radius-lg, shadow-lg, over the dialog scrim (neutral-900 at 38%) — the dialog’s own values, so the system still has one raised look. Header: title in the h4 face, IconButton (x) trailing, hairline under. Content area scrolls, space-4 padding.

Props title, onClose, closeLabel, children. States content scrolled (the hairline is the only thing under the header) · long title (wraps to two lines; nothing is pinned to a fixed header height, so German fits).

VoiceOver sheet: the scan’s date, announced as a dialog; close: “Close the PDF”.

F6

The five delivered screens — what the flows show that the kit does not

Not redrawn. This is the change list.

ScansScreen (S3)

  • Carries three subtitles; the flow has seven. Add “No pages yet”, “1 page — keep shooting”, “12 of 40 pages scanned”, “40 pages — PDF ready, photos deleted”.
  • No pressed row state is shown. The token exists (press-row, accent at 14% full bleed) but the screen never demonstrates it.
  • No ErrorLine slot above the list (S2’s storage sentence lands here too).
  • Row order is not stated as newest first.

CameraScreen (S6)

  • The shutter never goes disabled — the write moment (S7) is the rule that makes one press one page, and it is invisible in the kit.
  • Counter is shown at one value only; needs Page 1, Page 7, Page 40, plus the before-the-first-shot state where the footer button is disabled and reads “Photograph at least one page”.
  • No ErrorLine over the viewfinder (S9), and the four reasons are not wired to the one sentence shape.
  • The simulator stand-in note (S12) is not set on Viewfinder.
  • Shutter label is static; it must name the page: “Photograph page 7”.

PagesScreen (S15)

  • The thumbnail rail is drawn inline on the screen. It becomes PageStrip (F2) and gains the jump; at 40 pages the current rail is unusable.
  • “Adjust page” sits on the screen as a ghost button and also in the menu. The flows put it in the menu only — drop the duplicate.
  • Refused page (S17) shows the card but not the secondary action “Scan this page again” in the flow’s position.
  • The menu is one fixed list of four items; it is three while checking (S18) and four on a finished scan (S19).
  • “Make PDF” must be hidden, not disabled, until every photo has a page. Busy label “Making the PDF…” exists; the failure line (S22) does not.
  • The counter is drawn as “3 / 12” on the page and as “Page 3 of 12” in the bar. The flow’s copy is “Page 3 of 12” in both.

AdjustScreen (S23)

  • The six-tool strip is drawn on the screen. It becomes ToolStrip (F3).
  • Corner dragging for Edges and Crop is missing entirely; it becomes PageHandles (F4).
  • “Back to the suggestion” (ghost) is not present on the tools that have a suggestion.
  • The Edges note line has one state; it needs three (nothing to say, nothing to cut, the warning).
  • Neither apply path is shown: one page (S30, busy “Applying…”) or all pages (S31, full-screen ProgressLine), nor the skipped-pages line afterwards (S32).
  • Sharpen must be able to sit at 0, and 0 must read as no sharpening.

DoneScreen (S33)

  • TextField is shown empty only; needs focused and typed-in, and the two buttons must stay clear of the keyboard.
  • The photos-already-deleted state (S35) is not there: the destructive block and its footnote are removed, not greyed, and the shorter screen has to look finished.
  • “Open PDF” leads nowhere; it opens the Sheet (F5).
  • The footnote is attached to the button, not to the block; it belongs under the SectionLabel group so it survives the block being removed.
