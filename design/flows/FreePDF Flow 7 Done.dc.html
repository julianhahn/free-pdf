S34 — spec

Order app bar “PDF ready” · PageImage (the PDF’s first page, 150 px wide so the field and both buttons are above the keyboard) · space-4 · TextField · space-4 · Button primary “Open PDF”, space-2, Button primary “Share PDF” · then, when the keyboard is down, the quiet destructive block (see S35 for the shorter screen).

The keyboard while the field is focused the content above it scrolls, the two buttons stay on screen, and the destructive block is scrolled out rather than resized. What must never happen is Share sitting under the keyboard — the field is only useful because of the button under it.

Three field states empty shows the placeholder “scan” in text-muted, focused adds the 2 px accent ring 2 px outside the control, typed-in prints the name in text with the suffix “.pdf” trailing in text-muted. The suffix is not editable and not part of the value.

Nothing is stored reopen the scan and the field is empty again. There is no “saved” state and no confirmation of the name.

VoiceOver field: “Name for the shared copy. Text field. The shared copy is named scan dot pdf unless you type a name.” · buttons: “Open PDF. Shows the PDF in FreePDF.” / “Share PDF. Choose where a copy of the PDF goes.”

S35

Done, photos already deleted

The destructive block and its footnote are gone. Nothing greyed out, nothing left behind.
Open PDF Share PDF Change pages

S35 — spec

Order app bar · PageImage, now 230 px wide because there is room for it — the page is the thing worth looking at, so the shorter screen gives its space to the picture rather than leaving a hole where the block was · TextField · the three actions: Open, Share, then “Change pages” as a ghost.

Why it looks finished, not truncated the block is removed, not disabled, and no SectionLabel, hairline or footnote is left standing on its own. The screen ends on “Change pages”, which still works — the pages can be changed even with no photos, they just cannot be adjusted.

What is not said there is no “photos deleted” badge and no line about it here. The list already says it: “40 pages — PDF ready, photos deleted”.

VoiceOver as S34, minus the destructive control. Nothing announces the absence.

S36

Open PDF — the reader sheet

S36 — spec

Built from the proposed Sheet: surface, radius-lg, shadow-lg, on the dialog scrim, rising from the bottom edge. Header: the scan’s date and a close IconButton. Content: the PDF’s pages, scrolling continuously, pinch to zoom.

Nothing else on it no share button, no page count, no print, no “save to Files”. Nothing is copied and nothing leaves the phone by opening it.

Order and spacing header space-2 vertical / space-4 horizontal above a hairline · content space-4 padding, space-4 between pages · the sheet stops short of the top edge so the screen behind is still visible, which is what says “this closes”.

VoiceOver announced as a dialog named by the scan’s date; close: “Close the PDF”. The pages are read as images with their labels: “Page 1 of 40”.

S37

Share PDF — the moment before the phone’s own sheet

Open PDF Share PDF

Nothing leaves the phone until you choose where the copy goes.

Change pages

S37 — spec

Built from Button primary “Share PDF”. The sheet that follows is the phone’s own and is not designed here.

The one line a 13 px body line in text-muted directly under the button — not a dialog, not an alert, not an icon. It is the only place the app says anything about the network, and what it says is a fact: the copy is made when a destination is chosen, and until then nothing has moved. It sits under Share rather than at the top of the screen so it is read in the second before the tap.

The name what is shared is a copy named from the field (“Rental contract.pdf”); the scan itself keeps its date. An empty field shares “scan.pdf”.

VoiceOver “Share PDF. Choose where a copy of the PDF goes. Nothing leaves the phone until you choose.” The line is also read as text after the button, for someone reading rather than tapping.

The sentence is a placeholder — no wording for it exists in the copy tables.

Delete-the-photos confirmation

S38 — spec

The block behind it (this is S33’s lower half, shown because the dialog belongs to it) hairline, space-4, SectionLabel “The photos”, space-2, Button destructive with the count and the size, space-2, the footnote in 13 px text-muted. The footnote belongs to the block, not to the button, so removing the block (S35) removes the footnote with it.

The dialog ConfirmDialog: “Delete the 40 photos?” · “The PDF stays. Without the photos the pages can no longer be adjusted.” · “Delete photos” / “Cancel”. Asked every time; there is no settings screen to turn it off and no “don’t ask again”.

No red, and not colour alone the button names what goes and how much space it frees; it is accent-700 / accent-300; and it carries the double rule, so it is a different shape from the two primaries above it even before the colour is read.

VoiceOver destructive button: “Delete the 40 photos, 78 megabytes. The PDF stays. The pages can no longer be adjusted afterwards.” · dialog confirm: “Delete photos. Deletes the 40 photos and keeps the PDF.” · cancel: “Cancel. Keeps the photos.”
