S13

Scanning progress — early and late

S13 — spec

Order app bar (the scan’s date, back arrow — leaving is safe) · the block centred in the scroll area: ProgressLine with line, a 3 px bar, and note. Nothing else on the screen: no thumbnails appearing one by one, no page being previewed, no cancel — the run finishes or the app is closed.

Spacing the line to the bar and the bar to the note are ProgressLine’s own steps (space-2 / space-2); the block is centred vertically so it does not move as the sentence grows from “1 of 12” to “11 of 12”. Tabular figures keep the line the same width at both ends.

Both ends shown at 1 of 12 the bar is a short accent stub on a divider-strong track — it is visibly a bar, not an empty line; at 11 of 12 it stops short of the end, because the last page is not done yet.

The note is a fact “You can close the app. It carries on from here.” — the drain resumes after a kill. Distinguishing it from S31’s opposite note is done in S31, not here.

VoiceOver the region is a progress indicator: “Scanning page 11 of 12” updated as it counts, announced at most once a page; the note is read after it. The back arrow: “Back to the scan. The scan carries on.”

S14 — spec

Order app bar · ErrorLine at the top of the scroll area, with the cleanup’s own sentence · the ProgressLine block, still centred in what is left, still counting.

Why the bar keeps moving a refusal is one page’s outcome, not the run’s. The line stays on the page being worked on now (6 of 12), so the count never sits still while work is happening. The refused page is dealt with on the pages screen (S17), which is where it can be retried, retaken or deleted — nothing to decide here.

Several refusals the line is replaced, not stacked: one ErrorLine holds the most recent sentence, and the pages screen is where all of them are visible as refused tiles. Confirm the engine’s wording for a refusal during the run; the sentence shown is a plausible stand-in.

VoiceOver the refusal is announced once as an alert; the progress region keeps its own announcements. No control appears — there is nothing to tap.
