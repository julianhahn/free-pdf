S39 — spec

Built from the MenuList item on S19, then the S6 camera screen unchanged: Viewfinder, PageCounter onDark, Shutter, footer Button. The title is 41 because numbers keep their gaps and are never reused.

My call on the line a Viewfinder note, not an ErrorLine: “The PDF was removed. It is made again after this page.” Nothing failed, so the sentence does not belong in the shape reserved for the engine’s failures — an ErrorLine here would teach people to distrust the error line. Inside the frame it is read once, in the calm register the stand-in note already uses, and it disappears with the first shot.

Footer “Scan 1 page” — one photo waiting, so the count is 1, singular. Before the shot the footer is disabled and reads “Photograph at least one page”, exactly as S8.

VoiceOver the note is read after the counter on entry; shutter: “Photograph page 41.” The menu item that got here says what it costs: “Shoot another page. Photographs one more page at the end of this scan. The PDF is made again afterwards.”

The two consequences worth naming

The apply-to-all row. A kill mid-apply leaves a scan that is partly rewritten and, by design, unlabelled. That is why S31 says “Keep the app open.” and why it has no back arrow — the note is doing the work that a resume would otherwise have to do.

No screen shows a spinner on entry. Every landing screen has its content on the first frame, because the state is read from files rather than fetched. That is also what keeps the app free of skeletons, which the system forbids.
