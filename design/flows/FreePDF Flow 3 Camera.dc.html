Flow 3 — shooting the pages

S7 to S12. S6 is the delivered CameraScreen and is reviewed on the foundation sheet. The camera screen is the same frame every time: the title is the number the next shot lands on, the viewfinder is 3:4, the shutter sits where a thumb reaches, and the screen’s action is pinned in the footer.

FoundationFlows 1–2

S7

Camera, shutter disabled while the photo is written

A fraction of a second after each press. One press, one page.
Page 7
Scan 6 pages

S7 — spec

Order app bar (back arrow, title “Page 7”) · space-4 · Viewfinder with PageCounter onDark inset space-3 from its top-left corner · space-4 · Shutter disabled, centred · footer above a hairline: Button primary full width.

The disabled shutter uses the new colour role, not opacity (foundation F1): the paper disc stays at full --paper — the mass a thumb aims at does not fade — and the accent ring goes to --disabled-border. The press does nothing until the photo is on disk, which is what makes one press exactly one page.

Spacing space-4 between viewfinder and shutter, space-2 above the shutter row so the 44 pt target never touches the frame; footer padding space-4.

VoiceOver shutter, live: “Photograph page 7.” · shutter, writing: “Photographing page 7, wait” with the dimmed trait · footer: “Scan 6 pages. Cleans up the 6 photos and makes pages from them.” · counter: “Page 7. The next photo becomes page 7.”

S8 — spec

Built from the S7 frame with Shutter live and the footer Button disabled, reading “Photograph at least one page”. That label is an instruction, so it is set in the new disabled text colour, not at 45% opacity — the whole reason F1 exists.

The counter tabular figures at every value, so nothing shuffles between “Page 1” and “Page 40”; the ground changes, the type does not (onDark = viewfinder at 66% with a 6 px blur, the system’s one blur).

VoiceOver footer, disabled: “Photograph at least one page. Dimmed. Photograph a page before scanning.” · shutter: “Photograph page 1.”

S9

One sentence shape, the reason swapped in.

Page 7

S9 — spec

Order app bar · ErrorLine above the viewfinder, inside the scroll area · viewfinder (shorter, because the line took its space — the frame keeps 3:4 and shrinks, it is never cropped) · shutter · footer.

The line the engine’s sentence, printed unchanged: Page 7 was not saved: <why> Nothing already photographed is lost. The four reasons — out of storage, camera not ready, no photo handed over, camera stopped early — are swapped into the same shape and never rewritten. Shown here with the storage one.

The shutter stays live. The failure is about the last press, not the camera; pressing again is the retry, and the title still says Page 7 because the number was not used up.

VoiceOver announced once as an alert when it appears, then read in order above the viewfinder. No dismiss control.

S10

Camera, permission denied

The whole screen becomes the sentence and one button. No preview behind it.
Open Settings

S10 — spec

Built from EmptyState as the full-screen takeover shape — icon, the sentence as its title, and action = Button primary “Open Settings”. No body: there is one sentence and repeating it in smaller type would be padding.

Why the sentence is the title it is the only thing on the screen; setting it in the h4 serif is what makes it the screen rather than a warning attached to something else. It is also the one place the word “document” could appear — it does not here, because the copy table gives “the pages”.

Order and spacing app bar keeps the page number and the back arrow, so leaving is always possible · block centred in the scroll area · icon 26 px to title space-3, title to action space-4.

VoiceOver the sentence is read on entry; button: “Open Settings. Opens the iPhone’s settings for FreePDF, where the camera can be allowed.”

S11

Camera, the screen cannot work

Same shape as S10, with no action. Three sentences use it.
The other two sentences, same shape

S11 — spec

Built from EmptyState, no action. Identical layout to S10, which is the point: the user does not have to work out whether this one is fixable — if there is a button, there is something to do; if there is none, there is not.

Three sentences no camera hardware · the session failed to start · the stand-in failed (“Page 7 could not be drawn.”). Same icon, same position, no colour and no icon change between them — the sentence is the difference.

VoiceOver the sentence, read on entry. The back arrow is the only control, labelled “Back to the scan”.

S12 — spec

Built from Viewfinder with its note, nothing new. The note is the quiet line inside the frame, and it is the only thing that differs from S6 — the frame, the counter, the shutter and the footer are unchanged.

VoiceOver the note is read after the counter; the shutter says what it actually does here: “Draw page 7.”

Leaving and coming back (flow 9) every camera state above is a landing screen: the title is read off the files, so returning to an unfinished scan lands on the viewfinder at the next free number with no state carried in.
