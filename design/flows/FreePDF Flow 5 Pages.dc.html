S20

Delete-a-page confirmation

Page numbers keep their gaps afterwards and are never renumbered.
Make PDF

S20 — spec

Built from ConfirmDialog over the pages screen. Title, body, then the actions right-aligned: Button destructive “Delete page”, Button secondary “Cancel”.

After confirming the page and its photo go; the numbers keep their gaps, so the next page is still Page 4 and the title reads “Page 4 of 11”. There is no reorder and no insert-in-the-middle, on purpose — a number always means the same sheet.

VoiceOver “Delete this page? The photo goes too. This cannot be undone.” · confirm: “Delete page. Deletes page 3 and its photo.” · cancel: “Cancel. Keeps the page.”

S21

Pages, making the PDF

The pages stay visible and readable behind it.
Making the PDF…

S21 — spec

Built from Button with busy and the running label “Making the PDF…”. No overlay, no spinner, no takeover: the pages stay where they are and stay readable, because the wait is about a second and the screen has nothing to hide.

Busy is not disabled the button keeps its accent stroke and its label changes; presses are blocked while it runs. That is why the disabled fix does not touch it: nothing here is dead, something is happening.

The switch stays live Grey can still be flipped — it just makes the PDF again afterwards. If the engine forbids that mid-write, say so and the switch becomes disabled for the second it takes, using the F1 treatment.

VoiceOver “Making the PDF…” announced with the busy trait; when it finishes, the done screen is announced by its title.
